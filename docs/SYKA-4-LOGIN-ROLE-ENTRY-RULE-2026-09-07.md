# SYKA-4 Login Role Entry Rule — 2026-09-07

Login role selector behavior:

- **Pelajar**: show the `Masuk sebagai Peserta Kolektif` entry.
- **Guru**: hide the collective participant entry.
- **Penyelenggara**: hide the collective participant entry.

The collective participant portal remains available at `/peserta-kolektif/login`; this change only controls its visibility from the general login screen based on the selected account role.
